Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 August 2026  |  Applies to: our Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp Business number, and website chat/CRM system operated for The Vascular Center.

1. Who we are

The Vascular Center (Dr. Amol Lahoti), Century Hospital, Opposite Central Bus Stand, Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra, India, operates automated messaging on Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, and WhatsApp to answer patient questions and help schedule consultations.

2. Information we collect

When you message our Page, Instagram account, or WhatsApp number, or submit a form connected to our ads, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address, if you share them or they are provided by the platform (e.g. via a lead form).
  • The content of the messages you send us, including the language and menu options you select (e.g. condition type: pain, heaviness, swelling, varicose veins).
  • Your Messenger/Instagram/WhatsApp identifier (a platform-assigned ID), used only to reply to you and recognize returning conversations.
  • Which ad, page, or campaign brought you to us, if applicable.

3. How we use this information

  • To respond to your questions automatically and route you to the right information.
  • To help our clinic staff follow up with you regarding appointments or government-scheme eligibility.
  • To keep a record of patient inquiries for our internal CRM (customer relationship management) system.
  • We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

4. Where your data is stored

Message and lead data is stored in a secured database (Supabase) accessible only to authorized clinic staff. Automated reply logic runs on a workflow automation tool (n8n) that processes messages in order to generate a response and is not used to store data long-term.

5. Sharing with platforms

To deliver messages, we use the Meta Messenger Platform, Instagram Messaging API, and WhatsApp Business Platform. These platforms process message content solely to transmit it between you and our clinic, under Meta's own Platform Terms and Data Policy.
